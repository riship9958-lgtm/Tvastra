SHOTT

A family entertainment centre where every zone plays a different game.

The project

A high-energy playground of arcade, bowling and lounge, wrapped in concrete, timber and neon.

SHOTT is a family entertainment centre in Surat built around one idea, eat, party and play under a single roof. Behind a graphic, colour-blocked facade, the plan unfolds as a sequence of rooms, an arcade, a bowling alley, a kids’ world and lounges, each with its own character but tied together by a warm, raw material language.

Exposed-concrete shells and battened timber ceilings keep the base architectural and calm, while neon signage, pop-art murals and colour-blocked screens bring the energy. Astro-turf break-outs, redemption counters and glass-walled game rooms let each zone read clearly while the whole floor stays connected.
