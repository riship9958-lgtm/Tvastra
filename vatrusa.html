The inspiration

Where water meets oil.

The design of Vatrusa draws inspiration from the captivating interplay observed as petrol or oil converges with water — the fluid, unrepeatable patterns that form where the two blend together. That movement is translated into form, grain and line.

Available in Teak Wood, Oak Wood and Walnut Wood, each piece integrates seamlessly with the architecture and interiors around it — a comprehensive approach to transforming living spaces.
