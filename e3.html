E3 Bungalows

A luxurious family bungalow in grey marble, warm timber and glass.

The project

A contemporary family home where grey marble, warm timber and layered light set a calm, luxurious tone.

E3 Bungalows is a large family residence organised around open, light-filled living. Grey-veined marble floors run through a living and dining sequence framed by timber panelling, sheer drapes and full-height glazing that opens each room to the greenery and skyline beyond.

Sculptural coffered ceilings in timber and metal give the living spaces a strong signature, while bedrooms stay softer, in warm neutrals with jaali screens and accent panels. A generous terrace with a battened pergola extends the living outdoors, day and night.
